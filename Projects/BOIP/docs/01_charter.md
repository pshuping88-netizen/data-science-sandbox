# Project Charter

## Burrow Operations Intelligence Platform (BOIP)
Burrow Operations Intelligence Platform

___
## Project Overview
The Burrow Operations Intelligence Platform (BOIP) is a stock management system that forms the foundation of a broader Operations Intelligence Platform for The Burrow Coffee Shop. The platform digitises stock taking, centralises inventory records, maintains stock count history, and provides reporting tools that improve visibility into day-to-day operations.

Version 1 establishes the foundation of the platform by replacing manual stock sheets with a digital stock management system, providing the data required for future forecasting, business intelligence, and AI capabilities.
___
## Business Context
The Burrow Coffee Shop is a community-focused coffee shop established in 2018. Originally located alongside Luni Art Gallery in Salt River, the business now operates from Jubilee Community Church in Observatory, Cape Town.

The coffee shop currently manages inventory using manual stock sheets and Microsoft Excel, while Yoco is used for point-of-sale transactions. Discussions with management identified several operational challenges, including inconsistent stock counting, occasional stock shortages, limited tracking of wastage, and difficulty turning operational data into meaningful business insights.

BOIP is being developed to address these challenges by providing a centralised platform for managing inventory, monitoring operations, and improving decision-making.

___
## Project Vision
Develop an Operations Intelligence Platform that helps small businesses transform operational data into actionable insights, enabling more informed inventory management, operational planning, and business decision-making.

___
## Problem Statement
The Burrow Coffee Shop currently manages inventory using manual stock sheets and Microsoft Excel. While these tools support day-to-day operations, they can result in inconsistent stock counts, limited inventory visibility, occasional stock shortages, and over-ordering of perishable products such as fresh milk and koesisters.

Operational information is also spread across multiple sources, making it difficult to analyse inventory trends, monitor wastage, and generate meaningful business insights. As a result, opportunities to improve stock management and support operational decision-making are often missed.

___
## Project Objective
To design and develop a stock management system for The Burrow Coffee Shop that digitises inventory management, standardises stock taking, and provides reporting tools that improve inventory visibility and support operational decision-making.

___
## Project Scope
### Included (Version 1)

#### Inventory Management
- Manage products
- Maintain current inventory levels
- Monitor low-stock items

#### Stock Taking
- Record stock counts
- Maintain stock count history
- Update inventory levels after each stock count

#### Reporting
- Generate inventory summary reports
- View historical stock count reports
- Compare stock counts over time
- Summarise inventory by product category
- Display key inventory metrics

#### Documentation
- Project documentation
- Database design
- Data dictionary
- System architecture

___
### Not Included (Version 1)

- Computer vision stock counting
- Demand forecasting
- Recommendation engine
- Customer analytics
- Mobile application
- Cloud deployment
- Advanced AI models

___
## Success Criteria

Version 1 will be considered successful when:

- Products and inventory are managed digitally
- Stock counts are recorded consistently
- Inventory levels are updated after each stock count
- Low-stock items are identified automatically
- Inventory reports provide meaningful operational information
- Documentation is complete
- Project is published with a clear README

___
## Project Constraints

- Developed specifically around the operational requirements of The Burrow Coffee Shop
- Limited to manually entered operational data
- Developed using open-source technologies and free development tools
- Version 1 excludes predictive analytics and computer vision

___
## Future Enhancements
Potential future versions may include:

- Demand forecasting
